# Ethereal Veil Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

Ethereal Veil ("the app") is published by World Class Scholars / Christopher Appiah-Thompson.

## Data we collect

- **Studio sessions** — Drawings and paintings you save are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. Optional CloudKit sync uses your iCloud account when enabled.
- **Account preferences** — Sign-in state and access tier are stored on-device. We do not operate a separate account server.
- **Purchases** — Subscription status is processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details.

## Data we do not collect

- No advertising identifiers for third-party ad networks.
- No sale of personal data.
- No public social feed or user-generated content sharing.

## Third-party services

- **Apple** — App Store, iCloud, CloudKit, StoreKit.
- **archive.org** — Royalty-free classical music streams when you tap Play (HTTP/HTTPS as configured in the app).
